# collegeScorecard (development version)

* Corrected the documentation for `cost_avg` and `cost_avg_income_*` to describe net prices from both public and private institutions.
* Added `cost_attendance_academic_year` (`COSTT4_A`) and `cost_attendance_program_year` (`COSTT4_P`).
* Added `rate_completion_150_4yr` (`C150_4`) and `rate_completion_150_lt4yr` (`C150_L4`), without changing the existing on-time `rate_completion` measure.
* Clarified the existing Title IV school filter and the differing metric years in the Most Recent Institution bulk file.
